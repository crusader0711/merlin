**Document structure (from research roadmap):**

1. **Electromagnetic wave propagation** -- plane wave model, round-trip delay $\tau = 2R/c$, Doppler shift from moving target. Start from transmitted EM wave, show round-trip propagation, introduce delay and frequency shift.

2. **Radar range equation** -- derive from first principles: transmitted power density at range R, power intercepted by target (RCS $\sigma$), reflected power density back at receiver, power captured by receive aperture. Final form: $\text{SNR} = P_t G^2 \lambda^2 \sigma / ((4\pi)^3 R^4 k_B T_0 B_n F L)$. Show each step with physical interpretation.

3. **FMCW modulation** -- LFM chirp transmitted signal $s_t(t)$, received signal with delay and Doppler, dechirp mixing operation (multiply received by conjugate of transmitted). Show the mixing product explicitly.

4. **Beat frequency derivation (FULL form, CRITICAL)** -- derive $f_b = 2\mu R/c \pm f_d$ with BOTH range and Doppler terms. Use $f_d = 2v/\lambda$. The $+$ sign for approaching, $-$ for receding (convention $v > 0$ = approaching). This is the core equation. Do NOT simplify to stationary case first -- derive full form first per research anti-pattern guidance.

5. **Range equation** -- invert beat frequency for stationary targets: $R = cf_b/(2\mu)$. EXPLICITLY LABEL this as the "stationary-target approximation" with conditions for validity (when $f_d \ll 2\mu R/c$).

6. **Range resolution** -- $\Delta R = c/(2B)$ derived from minimum resolvable beat frequency difference.

7. **Velocity measurement** -- Doppler frequency across CPI: $v = \lambda f_d / 2$, extracted from phase progression across M chirps.

8. **Maximum unambiguous range and velocity** -- $R_\text{max} = c/(2f_r)$ from PRI constraint, $v_\text{max} = \lambda f_r / 4$ from Nyquist on Doppler sampling.

9. **Range-Doppler coupling analysis (PHYS-05)** -- Define coupling ratio as $f_d / (2\mu R/c)$. Show that for chirp rate $\mu = B/T_c$, the coupling depends on $T_c$. Evaluate symbolically for $T_{c,1} = 30~\mu\text{s}$ and $T_{c,2} = 0.5~\mu\text{s}$ (reference parameter_table.md for values). Show that short chirp has proportionally larger coupling. Discuss compensation approaches: 2D FFT processing (range-Doppler map), range migration correction for high-velocity targets.
