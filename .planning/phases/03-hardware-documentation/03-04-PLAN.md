8. **Emergency Stop** -- Document `Emergency_Stop()`: clears DAC5578 outputs via hardware CLR pin, disables all PA gate voltages immediately.

9. **References** -- Power Management V6.xlsx, main.cpp, DAC5578/ADS7830 datasheets, symbol_table.md, parameter_table.md.
